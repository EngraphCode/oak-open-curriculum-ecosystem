## Structure — the concept-exploration canonical movements

alternating metacognition/reason movements), not a parallel invention.
Exit criteria are named per movement before it runs
(`loop-exit-criteria-required`); a movement that merely restates its

### Movement 1 — raw observations (metacognition inward)

Gather what IS, without imposing a frame. Three evidence legs:

   floor): every surface that READS organisation or classification
   today, and the decision it makes. Enumeration targets (grown at
   read time, closed only when no new consumer class appears):

     model (the largest live consumer of "organisation");

### Movement 2 — problem space (reason outward)

State the gap, who it harms, mechanism, constraints, success — problem,
never solution. The **homonymy test** runs here as the discriminator:
for each candidate shared label, per lever (skill=summoned, rule=binds,
subagent=consulted), which movement-1 consumer decision would it drive?
Divergent decision surfaces per lever ⇒ the shared label does no work.
Constraints carried: ADR-221 (no parallel taxonomy), PDR-134 direction
law, PDR-009/051 axis separation, ADR-189 relation stated, the owner's
prior, the metadata-theatre gate with the null hypothesis (three light
per-corpus organisations) as the default to beat on NAMED decisions.
**Conservation constraint (owner word 2026-08-02, this sitting):
simpler "without compromising quality OR functionality"** — no candidate
(the null included) may regress what the levers currently DO: skill
summon-routing, always-applied rule loading, subagent dispatch, adapter
generation, vendored-external operation. Functional conservation is a
conjunct of the value gate, not a tiebreaker; omissions bear the burden
of proof.

**Exit criteria**: a problem statement the owner would recognise as his
commission; the homonymy verdict (one thing or three, with the
decision-surface evidence); the candidate set closed for movement 3.

### Movement 3 — solution-space reflection (metacognition inward)

Re-open the space: kind/stratum/intent (Falcon's candidate, preserved
as WS0 input), concern-centric, the null, and any shape movements 1–2
surface. Interrogate fluency explicitly: the recorded attraction to
concern-centric elegance; the inherited crystallised candidate; the
owner's prior. Each surviving candidate leaves this movement with its
value stated ONLY in terms of movement-1 decisions.

**Exit criteria**: each candidate has a named decision-value case and a
named falsifier, or is dropped with a recorded reason.

### Movement 4 — synthesis and recommendation

One holistic understanding; a recommendation with falsifiers per the
plan's WS0 contract, including what would have changed the verdict.
Pre-review before it reaches the owner: `assumptions-expert`
(proportionality/plan-readiness) + an Opus frame-challenger dispatch;
cricket pair (normal + adversarial) at this boundary. Routed to the
owner through the Director.

**Exit criteria**: the recommendation artefact exists, reviewed, with
unresolved evidence named; the owner can rule from it without reading
this record; every proposal states its functional-conservation case
(what currently works, and why it still works under the proposal).
