# Relationship map — the Parallax family and the established cognitive skills

R3 of the WS0 recommendation, delivered as an authored report at the
owner's partial ruling (2026-08-02). This answers the standing
question: how do the new skills relate to `metacognition`, `reason`,
`concept-exploration`, `free-play`, `proportionality`, and `plan`?
Evidence: both corpora read whole at the Skills seat during WS0
(all nine Parallax SKILL-CANONICALs, 1,081 lines; the RPIF report,
1,681 lines; the six established canonicals), recorded in
[the WS0 working record](skills-estate-organisation-ws0-working-record.md)
§WS6(c). Parallax is cited at the owner's tracked commit `5fa0b2a0a`.

**The one-paragraph answer**: the families are complementary, not
competing — the established skills are lighter, understanding-first
instruments; Parallax is the same underlying disciplines at a more
formal, artifact-heavy grain (Frame Cards, scale maps, run-status
vocabulary, evaluation suites). Nothing in Parallax is an entered
mode; every member is a workflow with a completion contract, so the
family fits the estate's existing three-layer composition vocabulary
(modes / workflows / programmes) without strain. The family is fully
self-contained — no summons edges into or out of the estate's skills —
and treats the surrounding Practice as an explicit host interface
(learning signals out, write-authority gated).

## What the relations mean in practice

- **Choosing between them**: reach for the established skills when the
  need is understanding, framing, or a decision in the flow of work;
  reach for Parallax when an inquiry needs durable artifacts,
  auditable independence, and formal depth control. The orchestrator's
  screening/core/standard/deep modes are a formalisation of exactly
  the sizing judgement proportionality makes conversationally.
- **Grain difference, not overlap conflict**: parallax-frame does not
  replace concept-exploration — one produces Frame Cards for a
  structured inquiry, the other produces a well-formed understanding
  before options exist. Running concept-exploration first and
  parallax on the surviving question is a natural composition.
- **Reverse-direction imports** (candidates the family offers the
  estate, held for WS6(d) integration): the audit independence
  classes ("an audit emulated by an orchestrator is always
  same-context self-review" is sharper than anything in our reviewer
  doctrine); parallax-learn's disposition vocabulary as a natural
  fit to the existing knowledge pipeline.
- **Summons edges**: none in either direction today, by design — the
  family is vendor-safe and relocatable (144 links audited, zero
  escapes). Any future summons edge (for example, proportionality
  citing the orchestrator's depth modes) is an estate-side authoring
  decision, not a family requirement.

## What this map does not decide

Structure (where the family lives on the file system, the category
tier, the family-bundle standard) is R1, held for the owner's
discussion — nothing here depends on or prejudges it. The map reads
content, not directories.
