# The Shared Model Under Two Practice Estates — Convergence on the Verification-Scarce Testimony Economy

## What this captures and why

On 2026-07-06 the owner, watching two sibling Practice estates (this repo — "oak"
— and the peer estate "resonance") each independently produce a major planning-estate
intervention in the same week, observed: *"I was surprised at the synchronicity between
the shapes and solutions between the two repos, and I believe they point to a
fundamentally shared model — but of what, I don't know."* This report answers **of
what**, secures the reasoning against context loss, and hands the load-bearing finding
to the inter-Practice protocol PDR as its "why" preamble. It is captured deliberately
rather than left in a session transcript because the whole arc it belongs to (the
memory-drain's *mechanism-without-legible-intent* finding → ADR-200's intent layer at
estate level) is about making intent legible; leaving the ecosystem-level intent in a
volatile transcript would be the same failure one level up.

## The two interventions being compared

- **Resonance** ran a ~30-hour *plan-estate re-founding*: mechanical freeze of the old
  estate with byte-identity proof; a line-level two-direction audit (every claimed-done
  item proven, every claimed-pending item probed for having already landed — result: 7
  of 7 divergences were *under-reported* completion); owner-ratified re-organisation
  around a destination (a verbatim north-star sentence + four pillars); re-authored plans
  with per-todo **typed proofs** (a closed six-kind taxonomy: artifact / gate / probe /
  git-fact / ratified / attested); a conservation ledger whose 223 rows *tile* all 1,443
  inventory lines with zero gaps and zero overlaps; a fresh-context adversarial challenge
  of **all** mappings including the clean ones (zero overturns); and repoint-before-retire
  in one atomic commit. Doctrine home: resonance PDR-129 (recomputable plan state),
  PDR-130 (recomputable team state).
- **Oak** ratified **ADR-200**: the planning estate is rewritten onto a living
  **idea-graph** — ideas as JSON-LD nodes are the single source of truth, documents become
  co-equal human projections joined by frontmatter typed edges; two drift mechanisms (a
  deterministic frontmatter↔store validator and a semantic prose↔frontmatter reconciliation
  workflow wired as an active gate); a two-direction no-loss audit run by a fresh-context
  reviewer that did not perform the harvest; history-preserving evolution ops
  (supersede, never overwrite); closed **registered vocabularies** for every organising
  axis (no free text); and an entailed future family of repo knowledge-graphs.

Different objects (plan-state truth vs idea conservation), different physical designs (a
proof taxonomy vs a graph), yet the same twelve mechanisms recurred across both (recorded
state as a cache checked in both divergence directions; one-canonical-source; conserve-then-retire
with mechanical freeze rules; challenge-the-clean adversarial verification by fresh context;
destination-over-provenance organisation; YAML frontmatter as the machine layer;
supersede-never-overwrite with provenance chains; closed typed vocabularies; decision-complete
delegate briefs with refusal clauses and 100%-verification; "plans encode beliefs, gates encode
facts"; TTL/expiry on any hand-asserted state; fluency-as-hazard doctrine).

## The finding: a model of the verification-scarce testimony economy

A nine-lens panel (workflow run `wf_da1d87e0-156`; eight disciplinary lenses reading the
primary sources blind, plus one deflationary adversary) was run to answer *"a model of
what?"*. **Eight independent lenses, each in its own vocabulary, named the same underlying
structure**, and even the deflationary adversary conceded the same residue.

> **The shared model is a model of the verification-scarce testimony economy: the problem
> that arises whenever a body of knowledge must be kept true by many cheap, fallible,
> ephemeral producers whose failure mode is confident fabrication rather than silence, over
> one external record that is their only shared memory, under one scarce authority.**

That problem has been solved independently, by mature human disciplines, at least eight
times; each estate is re-deriving the *general form* of the solution. The lens convergence:

| Lens | Names the shared model as… |
| --- | --- |
| Accounting / audit | Accountability for a stock you cannot see (the stewardship problem): the ledger, the external auditor, the audit committee |
| Database theory | Derived-state truth-maintenance over a store with no integrity engine — a DBMS whose clients confabulate instead of crashing |
| Build systems / compilers | Correct incremental recomputation over an untrusted, non-hermetic derivation ("a plan is a lockfile of beliefs") |
| Distributed systems | State-machine replication for ephemeral nodes with a Byzantine fault mode (fluent overclaim), errors *correlated* because every node runs the same model |
| Epistemology of science | A knowledge-producing institution under testimonial collapse: replication, disinterested review, provenance, retraction-not-erasure |
| Evolutionary biology | Heredity for an amnesiac lineage near Eigen's error threshold: split an authoritative germline from disposable projected phenotype, or lose your truth to Muller's ratchet |
| Institutional economics | The Weberian office at its limit — total turnover per task, so all memory, trust, and succession law must live in the files |
| Cybernetics / control | Homeostasis of a truth-record under entropy, run by disposable controllers: an observer/estimator loop where sensing (verification) is dear and actuation (asserting) is free |

None is the parent; several named the root in the same words — *maintaining warranted belief
among mutually-inaccessible, ephemeral agents where assertion is cheap and verification is
dear.* Accounting, databases, distributed systems, bureaucracy, and science are **sibling
instances** of that one structure. LLM agent estates instantiate it in unprecedented purity
because they push every parameter to a limit no prior medium reached (turnover → total,
generation cost → ~0, producer reliability → fluent-overclaim, shared memory → a file store).

## The confound resolution (the owner's actual question)

The owner named himself as a possible confound. He is one — *and* the surprise is correctly
calibrated, because he is one of **three nested generators**:

1. **Shared minds** (the owner + one model family) — explains the shared *aesthetics and
   wording* (near-identical clauses like *"a second copy is a second drift surface"*: one
   author writing under two names, invisible to the person who never sees the two prose
   streams side by side).
2. **Shared axioms** (resonance's practice-core was transplanted from oak) — explains
   *parallel derivation* from common ancestry. This is strong and observed: ~88 of ~100 rule
   files are name-identical; the transplant landed 2026-07-03, **48 hours before** the
   re-founding; and resonance's flagship PDR-129 *names its own ancestor in its Related block*
   — the oak rule `validators-must-recompute-not-just-record` (created 2026-05-06), which had
   just arrived. The deflationary case is genuine and must not be softened.
3. **Shared conditions** — the niche both estates inhabit. This is what the surprise points at.

The confounds fully and correctly explain the shared *axioms*, the shared *vocabulary*, the
*search speed*, and the *physical form* (frontmatter). What survives them — the residue the
deflationary adversary itself conceded — is four things:

1. **Failure-first construction.** The mechanisms were derived from *measured local incidents*
   (resonance's 7/7 under-reported completions; oak's own false-greens and wrong-authority
   plans) that occurred **after** the shared axioms had already failed to prevent them. A
   transplanted axiom that did not stop the drift cannot be the cause of the cure's specific
   shape.
2. **Convergence on the culturally non-salient form.** Both estates guard
   `recorded-pending-but-green` — the direction *every CI culture in the training corpus
   omits*. Mimicry of training data reproduces the one-directional folk guard; deriving the
   *symmetric* guard from local evidence is the environment selecting, not the prior retrieving.
3. **Divergent phenotype, convergent constraint.** The idea-graph and the six-kind proof
   taxonomy *share no structure* yet obey the same invariants (closed vocabulary, recomputable,
   expiring, two-direction). In biology's terms this is the fingerprint of **analogy** (shared
   niche), not **homology** (shared ancestor). The owner has correctly netted out the homology
   and is looking at the analogy — and analogy, by construction, points at the environment.
4. **The cross-domain clincher.** The mechanisms match what aviation instrumentation,
   14th-century accounting, Popperian science, and Bazel each converged on **independently,
   sharing no owner, no model, and no transplant** with either estate. Three-plus independent
   arrivals at one structure is evidence the structure is real.

The single cleanest discriminator: **the same owner and same model, applied to these repos'
*other* problem classes (curriculum graphs, search quality, design systems), produced
*non-*converging artefacts.** Convergence concentrates exactly and only where the problem class
is identical — regulating a symbolic record against a world with cheap, unreliable writers. If
it were owner taste or model style, everything would rhyme; it rhymes only where the niche is
shared.

Held as a model, not a possession: *the current reading is that the convergence is homology in
the axioms and analogy in the mechanisms, driven by a real shared niche.* What would change
this reading is the falsifier below.

## The falsifier (an empirically-decidable experiment)

Every lens independently proposed the *same* falsifier, and that unanimity is itself
significant: **a third estate — different owner, different model family, no transplant
ancestry, but the same conditions (sustained multi-session agentic work over a file store).**
If it independently grows the same organs (two-direction recompute gates, challenge-the-clean
verification, absence-re-derivation, supersede-not-overwrite), the niche is the driver and the
shared model is real. If it thrives long-term on hand-asserted status and mutable history, the
niche story weakens and the confound explanation was sufficient. The inter-Practice protocol's
tier ladder provides this natural experiment the moment a third estate joins — which makes
*"what is the shared model?"* an empirically decidable question, not a philosophical one.

## Why this matters for "bring together ecosystem and estate; keep enriching each other"

The lenses do not merely name the structure; they prescribe the design.

**The owner's triad (state / intent / history) maps onto the shared model's three planes,
and the shared model dictates the truth-policy for each:**

- **Intent** — the durable germline / chart of accounts / canonical register: portable,
  one-canonical-source. Oak's idea-graph is this plane.
- **State** — the sensed-and-recomputed plane: **recomputable edges** (see the unification
  below), checked in both divergence directions, each axis choosing *project-from-its-system-of-record*
  vs *recompute-from-repo-facts* by who owns the truth, and expiring.
- **History** — the append-only journal / audit trail: supersede-never-overwrite, provenance
  chains. Multiple lenses independently flagged history-as-*queryable* (not merely
  append-only) as the **next forced invention** and the plane both estates have built least;
  oak's corpus/comms instrument is its natural lever.

**The unification — proofs are edges.** A resonance typed proof (`probe` / `gate` / `git-fact`
/ …) is a machine-checkable edge from a state claim to the ground facts that realise it, *with
a recompute procedure attached*. An oak `realised_by` / `validated_by` edge is a pointer from
an idea to where it became real — a proof *without its recompute procedure yet*. They are the
same construct seen from two sides. Oak's WS2 idea-node schema should therefore define **one
primitive — the recomputable edge** — of which acceptance proofs, realisation edges, and
evidence edges are all instances. That single move fuses both estates' inventions and gives the
triad one grammar. Design it **content-addressed** from day one (multiple lenses predict full
recomputation will not scale and incremental/dirty-tracking is forced) — which also cures the
proof-rot and PDR-renumber classes structurally.

**"Keep enriching each other" has a precise name in the shared model: recombination.** The
exchange is not a courier service; it is the anti-ratchet mechanism (biology's sex; epistemology's
inter-lab warrant transfer) by which two error-accumulating lineages repair each other's drift.
That is *why* both estates ruled it a first-class standing strand on 2026-07-06. Two structural
consequences follow, and both are already in the queue: a **conserved shared-core vocabulary vs
freely-extensible accessory** split (biology's Dobzhansky-Muller incompatibility; this *is* the
WS0e shared-schema + version-family-compat contract — the biology lens predicted the owner's own
queued work from first principles), and **typed cross-estate provenance** (which estate first
warranted a claim; how warrant crosses the boundary).

## What both estates are forced to invent next (convergent predictions)

The lenses' predictions were strikingly concordant; the concordance is itself evidence. Two
already sit in oak's queue (marked ★):

- ★ **A conserved shared-core / accessory vocabulary split** and version-family compatibility
  (= WS0e).
- ★ **Incremental recomputation with watermarks** — recompute-since-last-green keyed on content
  hashes; full recompute demoted to a periodic deep audit (shapes the recomputable-edge primitive).
- **Gates for the gates** — a scheduled sensor-health regime that re-certifies every gate and
  reviewer with injected known-bads (both estates hold the mutation-probe seed).
- **Materiality + risk-tiered verification** — a closed, machine-checked stakes taxonomy
  calibrating verification depth to risk (verification cost is now *measured* — ~1.3M subagent
  tokens for resonance's r6 waves).
- **Verifier independence codified** — the producer of work may never author the verifier's
  brief; a conflict-of-interest / independence ledger.
- **A formal recombination/merge protocol** beyond one-way teaching, with typed cross-estate
  provenance of warrant.
- **History made queryable** — typed history edges (session lineages, exchange correspondence,
  supersession chains) over the append-only substrate.

## Portable core (donor-neutral — the intended protocol-PDR preamble)

> Two peer Practice estates, evolving independently, converge on the same knowledge-maintenance
> mechanisms because they are two instances of one underlying structure: **the verification-scarce
> testimony economy** — maintaining warranted belief among many cheap, fallible, ephemeral
> producers whose failure mode is confident fabrication, over one external record that is their
> only shared memory, under one scarce authority. Every mature human institution for keeping
> knowledge trustworthy — double-entry bookkeeping, the scientific method, the database, distributed
> consensus, the bureaucratic office, the control-system observer — is a domain-specific instance
> of the solution to that problem. The inter-Practice protocol exists because horizontal exchange
> between estates is **recombination**: the anti-ratchet mechanism by which two error-accumulating
> lineages repair each other's drift and keep converging on truth. That is why the exchange is a
> first-class standing relationship, not a one-off transfer.

This paragraph is written donor-neutral so it may be lifted verbatim into the protocol PDR's
preamble in *both* estates (each estate names the other as "the Practice donor / peer estate"
per the neutral-vocabulary doctrine).
