## The causal stack

**Technical root.** The estate's single-lesson graduations were
QUEUE-COUPLED to whole-buffer rotation. The sharpest specimen is the
exit-code false-green class: first captured ~2026-07-17, held in per-user
memory and napkin entries as "route to the next curation pass" — and then
re-bitten at least three more times by three further seats (dated napkin
entries 2026-07-19 and two on 2026-07-20) before this pass spent roughly ten
minutes turning it into an estate-wide rule with adapters. Graduating that
one lesson never needed a frozen napkin corpus, a stable read window, or a
dedicated curator seat; it needed ten minutes and a nameable home, both
available to every seat that re-hit it. The same coupling held other
single-instance-sufficient lessons (the dormancy cursor-init contract, the
claims-heartbeat bundling) in the buffer across the same window.

**Process root.** Session-completion closeouts across the window each
correctly declared "dedicated consolidation DUE" — and each treated that
declaration as the ONLY graduation vehicle, citing the frozen-corpus
constraint ("the buffer holds other seats' unprocessed entries needing a
stable read/diff window"). The constraint is real — for ROTATION. It was
applied to graduation wholesale. PDR-100's single-instance graduation and
the distilled surface's own promote-on-first-instance doctrine already
licensed immediate homing; nothing in the closeout ritual asked "which of
tonight's captures could graduate NOW?"

**Meta root (where the stack stops).** Deferral-to-the-dedicated-pass reads
as rigour — the same comfort the drive generator exploits at authoring time
(a closed, complete-sounding claim) appearing in the learning loop as a
closed claim about WHERE graduation may happen ("the dedicated pass owns
graduation"). Batch-consolidation is genuinely superior for cross-seat
synthesis, so the frame is right often enough to feel right always. The next
"why" (why complete-sounding process claims soothe) leaves the estate's
control.

## The counterfactual test (segments of the same arc)

- **Cured segment**: this pass graduated the exit-code class in one sitting
  — rule, four adapter forms, index row — inside a session that also did
  everything else. Unit cost: minutes. The recurrence cost of the six-day
  wait: at least three further false-green incidents at landing boundaries,
  each risking a false completion claim (one push rejection and one commit
  failure were caught only by in-band checks).
- **The batching counterexample, kept honest**: the seven-firing generator
  graduation could NOT have happened per-session — no single seat held the
  cross-seat corpus view; the verification-methods pattern needed four
  seats' instances; the union-dedup needed the frozen boundary. The
  dedicated pass earned its keep exactly where synthesis was the substance.
- **The staging near-miss** (same arc, opening segment): the
  Director-staged worktree was cut from `main` while the live memory estate
  rode the coordination branch — the stale-capture-wins class aimed at the
  consolidation session itself, by its own staging brief. Caught in the
  session's first minutes only because grounding re-derived the base
  first-hand instead of trusting the brief; the Director's approval
  ("your verdict corrects my staging call") confirmed the catch. A staged
  brief is a pre-authorisation, and pre-authorisation wants its recompute
  condition attached — the drive retrospective's kept free-play seed,
  enacted by this session unknowingly before reading it.
- **The wait segment**: between READY (17:42Z) and slot grant (19:07Z) the
  seat idled ~85 minutes on a Director processing gap that overlapped a
  token false-alarm and the queue wedge. The system self-corrected (the
  Director's catch-up swept the window), and the seat's READY carried no
  ping step — the wedge reporter's one-cadence ping (a peer, same window)
  is the modelled cure.

## Honest credit

The dedicated-pass model bought what dribbled graduation could not: the
generator synthesis, the four-seat verification pattern, three registers at
zero simultaneously, the wrap-family import done coherently with its
reference graph swept, and a single curator judgment over a frozen corpus
(the union-duplication resolved at rotation rather than compounding). The
session also stress-tested its own imports same-day: wrap ran the closeout,
and the settled-round predicate governed the PR that carried it. The cost
side is equally real: six days of graduation latency for fast-lane lessons,
paid in recurrences.

## Proposals (warrant + falsifier + PDR-130 lane, each)

1. **[FAST — enacted with this record] Graduation latency is a recurrence
   cost: single-lesson graduations decouple from the dedicated pass.** At
   any session close, a captured lesson that (a) is single-instance
   sufficient (PDR-100), (b) has a nameable doctrine home, and (c) needs no
   cross-seat synthesis, graduates THEN — the frozen-corpus constraint
   gates rotation and cross-seat synthesis only. Home: the
   `consolidate-until-done` grounding bridge and `session-handoff`'s
   capture step. *Warrant*: the exit-code specimen above. *Falsifier /
   prediction (PDR-130 line)*: captured-lesson recurrence between capture
   and homing should drop to ~zero for clause-eligible lessons; if
   per-session graduations instead produce collision or duplication churn
   exceeding the recurrence cost within a quarter, the batching frame wins
   and the clause retracts.
2. **[FAST — enacted with this record] A staged brief carries a base
   recompute at pickup.** Any pre-staged session input (a cut worktree, an
   input manifest, a pre-named base) is a hypothesis whose BASE the picking
   session re-derives first-hand against the live memory estate before
   substantive work. Home: `consolidate-until-done` §Required Grounding.
   *Warrant*: the staging near-miss above. *Falsifier*: if the check never
   fires across a quarter of staged sessions, it is ceremony — fold it into
   the general grounding line and stop naming it.
3. **[FAST — enacted with this record] A READY report to a quiet routing
   seat gets one ping after one cadence.** Not a self-granted default, not
   an escape hatch — the wedge reporter's modelled move: a single directed
   ping naming the unacked contract, then hold. Home:
   `route-blocks-and-questions-to-director`. *Warrant*: the 85-minute idle
   segment against the peer's same-window ping that surfaced the Director
   gap. *Falsifier*: if pings routinely land during normal grant latency
   (noise), lengthen the cadence or retract.
4. **[OBSERVATION, routed not legislated] The single-owner-interface stall
   class.** A Director processing gap serialises every seat's owner-facing
   contract; PDR-063's owner-absent deadline-and-default machinery exists
   for retirement authority but nothing equivalent exists for routing
   contracts, and any extension must not become a grant bypass
   (no-escape-hatches). Routed to the Director/owner as a design question
   with the tension named; no proposal here.
