# Concept intake

**Authority:** discovery input only; no doctrinal or implementation authority

This lane makes potentially useful OCE concepts visible early enough to review, combine, test, reject or promote deliberately. Its documents are discovery catalogues, not plans, decisions, requirements or implementation authority.

## Rules

- Express every concept in OCE-native language as a standalone idea.
- Use a stable concept ID, status `candidate`, significance, boundary or non-goal, and a falsifier or cheapest discriminating probe where the entry is developed enough to support one.
- Preserve materially distinct alternatives and tensions; merge only true semantic duplicates.
- Group entries by OCE theme, not by the circumstances of discovery.
- Do not promote a candidate by recurrence, apparent popularity or inclusion in this lane.
- Move a concept into a plan, decision, Practice surface or implementation only through that surface's normal authority and evidence process.
