## The three-layer plan hierarchy (owner: ratified in principle)

The corpus already grows this hierarchy ungoverned (per-collection roadmaps;
"controlling" / "child" / "`.execution.plan`" self-labels); Walk A ratifies a
contract rather than inventing a layer:

| Layer | Count prior | What it is | External mapping |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Vision / strategy | exists | `VISION.md` + `docs/strategy` — lanes derive from it (r2) | — |
| Lane roadmap | one per lane + one estate | direction, sequencing, the lane's strategic plans with states; never executable detail | Linear Initiative / future graph node |
| Strategic plan | **~20 total** (owner count) | an OUTCOME with warrant, acceptance, decision record, and its own continuity; the unit of owner sanction; lives until the outcome lands | Linear Project |
| Implementation plan | as required; **always parented** | an executable slice with todos and gates — the unit an implementer seat picks up; born from a strategic plan, archives with provenance at landing | Linear issues / cycles |

State flows UP: implementation completion recomputes strategic-plan state;
strategic states roll into roadmap views (the R0b plan-state engine's
derivation, given its proper shape). The always-parented rule is the
structural control against orphan-effort accretion: no implementation work
may exist without a live strategic parent.

## Counts and the WIP limit (owner-reacted)

- **Intent lanes: 6–8** (owner: "6 is good, 10 is probably too many").
- **Roadmaps: one per lane + one estate roadmap** (owner: sensible).
- **Strategic plans: ~20 total**, of which a hard WIP limit of **5 in
  execution** with **2–3 owner-hot** at any moment (the rest agent-warm,
  owner appearing at gates only). The limit binds OWNER ATTENTION at
  sanction/gate moments, not agent throughput — agents parallelise INSIDE
  strategic plans via implementation plans and seats. Worked instance
  (2026-07-14): ~15 seats and 7 concurrent PRs were in truth 2–3 strategic
  streams, serialised for owner attention by the Director; the felt chaos
  was a labelling failure, not a work failure.
- Queued strategic plans (~15) hold recomputable state — visible and
  sequenced, never deleted, not taxing attention.
- The metric above any count: the live set must be small enough to hold in
  one mind.

## Thread semantics (Director recommendation; Walk A decides)

Threads today conflate continuity with work-containment (12 active + 14
paused against one human developer; the owner: "define what they mean
better… what's a sensible WIP limit? It's not 10").

- **Recommended: dissolve** — continuity becomes a section OF the strategic
  plan (identity chain, next-session landing, lane state; the V0.1 form
  already carries state), plus ONE Director map for cross-plan session
  awareness. "Thread" survives as a concept — the continuity dimension of a
  plan — not as an artefact family.
- **Conservative fallback:** threads redefined strictly 1:1 with
  in-execution strategic plans, making active-thread count equal the WIP
  limit by construction.
- Residual case either way: non-plan continuity (external research arcs,
  standing concerns) — the conservatory lane can hold these.

## Homeless concepts (owner agreed)

"Homeless" decomposes into three kinds with three cures:

1. **Wrong-kind content** (operational records, evidence, research inside
   the plans tree) re-homes BY FUNCTION, never into a lane — the 2026-07-14
   six-register re-homing is the worked instance.
2. **Valuable-but-unplaced intent** enters the **conservatory lane**: each
   entry in destination-quality form + provenance to the frozen source + a
   PLACEMENT TRIGGER naming what future lane or decision would home it.
   The lane's existence removes force-fit pressure; triggers plus Walk-C
   review stop it becoming a second corpus.
3. **Historical/completed concepts** conserve in the frozen archive with
   provenance edges — the machinery already guarantees this floor.

## Analysis granularity (owner agreed)

Three units, one per layer of the machinery:

- **Proof: the block/row** — tiling arithmetic accounts for every line of
  the old corpus (carried / archived / superseded-with-provenance).
- **Judgement: the concept** — recognised ACROSS files; the same idea in
  five plans becomes one destination statement with five provenance edges
  (many-to-one is what tiling + provenance support). This is what makes the
  work reconstruction, not re-filing.
- **Authoring: the destination plan** — co-authored fresh in V0.1 form from
  recognised concepts; never an edited copy.

Concept-recognition quality is the arc's judgement bottleneck — the reason
the challenge streams (OG-2 machinery half) and the sealed canary key (OG-3)
gate R3 before any rollout.
