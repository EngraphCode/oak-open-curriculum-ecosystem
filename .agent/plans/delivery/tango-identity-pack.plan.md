ruled on 2026-08-17 (seven design rulings at the critical-analysis sitting;
captured at occurrence in comms event `ec2c307b` and the

## Goal

When this lands, "identity as configuration" is a shipping mechanism rather
than a thesis: an identity is a self-contained validated package the kit
knows how to consume, adding one is a data act the identity-№N regression
proves, brand admission is machine-checked at the manifest (P3, through G1's
instrument), and the first identity delivered through that mechanism — Tango
— renders at the wow bar across all four themes. Today none of this exists:
the kit exports only its own CSS/JS surface (verified:
`packages/design/oak-design-system/package.json` exports), the
counter-identity sheets are kit-resident sources under
`studio-source/whitelabel/` served as validator-guarded copies in
`demos/oak-design-showcase/public/brands/`, and the identity roster is
hand-maintained across seven demo source surfaces (the product union and
labels in `components/useIdentity.ts`, the fail-loud slug→fragment map and
`TargetIdentity` union in `lib/identities.ts`, the test-estate roster in
`tests/apply-state.ts`, the per-identity expectation table in
`tests/fluid-type-fixture.ts`, the white-labelling page's records, the
specimen's served-path convention, and the kit-asset parity manifest).

## Mechanism

**The pack.** Each identity is its own workspace under
`packages/design/identities/` (exact package naming settled at T1a within
the `@oaknational` scope). The path mints a new directory tier against the
ratified workspace-reorganisation programme — named on the ratification
card with its enforcement warrant: the per-pack licensing surface (M1,
DDR-005) is a boundary the flat tier cannot express. A pack contains:

- **`manifest`** — a zod-validated data file (plain JSON — deliberately NOT
  a DTCG document; where a pack contributes real DTCG tokens those ride
  their own token files, and any pack-specific extension data inside them
  carries the pinned vendor key `org.oaknational.identity-pack` under
  `$extensions` per the DTCG 2025.10 format). The manifest declares the
  identity's slug, display name, description, and specimen target fragment
  (the data the demo roster derivation consumes); its theme roster; its
  slot bindings and expression-layer selector surface; its asset table;
  its licensing declaration; and the machine-checkable facts below.
- **`brand CSS`** — authored, not generated, over the kit's published brand
  surface, **plus the pack's themed authoring surface**: the pack declares
  its own `[data-theme='high-contrast']` / `[data-theme='colour-safe']`
  (and dark) blocks so P5's authored access themes are expressible. Today
  the kit hardcodes those themes to private Oak primitives behind
  specificity guards and declares them non-brand surface — a one-time,
  dated kit contract amendment (named T1c work) opens the per-theme
  authoring path for packs, winning on equal specificity + later source
  order, without weakening the kit's own defaults for packless consumers.
- **`expression layer` (declared)** — a pack may carry expression rules
  targeting **kit-contract selectors only**; demo-specific class rules stay
  demo-side. The manifest declares the expression layer's selector surface
  so admission can see it (today's counter-identity sheets mix kit-surface
  custom properties with demo-class rules — the pack shape separates them).
- **`assets`** — vendored, offline-safe, licence notices beside them, each
  asset row declaring its **role**: decorative (CSS-deliverable) or
  meaning-bearing (requires a DOM-side text alternative; CSS delivery alone
  cannot carry one — SC 1.1.1 — and forced-colours does not repaint
  background imagery).
- **`licensing surface`** — the pack's own licence statement per
  licence-follows-provenance (DDR-005). A per-pack licence surface is a new
  precedent beside the kit's `LICENSING-MANIFEST.md`; the pointer row /
  dated DDR-005-consistent amendment is named T1a work, never implied.

**Manifest facts (the machine-checkable floor).** Machine-checked, each
proven red-first — facts 1–5 and 8 at the schema with rejection
fixtures; facts 6 and 7 are CSS predicates checked by the T1b guard
arms over pack CSS (a manifest field asserting them would be a
self-attestation that records rather than validates — dated true-up
2026-08-17, pre-execution review):

1. **All four token-bearing themes present as full peers** — light, dark,
   high-contrast, colour-safe (DDR-004: full peers, additions land in all
   four or not at all). `system` is refused as a declarable theme — it is
   a control-surface resolution, not a token tree; consumer controls still
   present all five selections (DDR-004's subset rule).
2. **Per-theme declared polarity** (`color-scheme`) — the data W3's
   flash-safety falsifier and the print ink-safe revert both consume; and
   admission refuses polarity levers at selectors more specific than
   `:root`, which would silently beat `print.css`'s `html:root` revert.
3. **Per-theme probe target** — the theme-application proof's
   distinctiveness target as pack data, curing the recorded vacuity hazard
   (`theme-proof.ts` documents that a pack default equal to a probe
   target makes theme cells pass with the theme block dead, and delegates
   the guard to brand admission).
4. **Motion stance via the `-full` token surface only** — admission
   refuses bare `--motion-*` declarations at `:root`, the documented
   recipe that defeats `prefers-reduced-motion` inside the kit's collapse
   media query (live today in a counter-identity sheet).
5. **Target-size floor declared** — `--size-target` is brand-writable with
   no instrument; the manifest declares the pack's floor at or above
   WCAG 2.5.8's 24px minimum (kit bar: 44px), schema-refused below it.
6. **`forced-color-adjust: none` prohibited** in pack CSS — one opt-out
   invalidates the estate's zero-opt-out forced-colours scoping argument.
7. **Focus-ring surface untouched** — the kit's ring recipe is not on the
   brand surface ("never restyle it away"); admission makes that prose
   machine-checked for packs.
8. **Fixed-point declarations** — any whole-slot shorthand (the R14
   cascade opt-out) requires a declared fixed-point row with its reason:
   the named register, machine-homed.

**Admission (P3 through G1).** One instrument, not two: the ratified W1
slice B guard (G1) gains the manifest-driven arms above. It operates over
the pack's **import closure**, not per-file (both existing counter-identity
sheets split declarations across an `@import`ed sibling; the repo's
postcss closure walker in `kit-asset-parity.ts` is the reuse precedent),
and derives the slot↔parts relation from the kit's own slot definitions
(the parts of slot S are the custom properties the kit's S references) so
the check self-maintains as slots land. Every arm proves it bites on a
seeded fixture before it guards anything.

**The kit consumption surface, both R16 shapes.**

1. **Static single-identity** (the real-app shape): an app imports the kit
   base plus **exactly one pack** — zero bytes from any *other* pack are
   served. Honest bound, stated: the kit base carries Oak's own slot
   values today, so "completely swapped" means the pack's sheet wins the
   cascade over the base, not that Oak's bytes are absent; the value-free
   base is the Oak-pack migration node's outcome, not this one's. The
   proof consumer is a minimal fixture app (T1c) — no static
   single-identity consumer exists today.
2. **Demo runtime switching** (the demo-equipment shape): a
   **build-time-generated roster module** (zod-parsed at module init, the
   demo's existing precedent) derives from installed pack manifests and
   feeds every roster surface — the product union and labels, the
   slug→fragment map, the served-path rows with their parity/closure
   guard, **and the test-estate matrices**: the a11y matrix derives from
   the same generated roster, so the data act that makes an identity
   visible is the act that makes it gated — a pack structurally cannot
   render without cells. Asset and sheet URL resolution is answered at
   T1c for both shapes (sheet-relative `url()` under the demo's
   `<link>` serving; bundler-resolved under package import — the kit's own
   DTCG export excludes icon-URL tokens for exactly this
   environment-relativity reason, and the emission reuses
   `design-tokens-core`'s existing DTCG→CSS emitter for a narrow
   asset-property layer; no expressive-value generator — see §Out of
   scope).

**The identity-№N regression (K2).** The zero-edit property is the END
STATE the one-time re-plumb creates, and the plan says so: T1d converts
the seven hand-maintained roster surfaces to the generated module, and
FROM THEN ON adding a pack requires zero framework or demo source edits —
proven red-first by a deliberately partial fixture pack rendering via
declared role-level fallbacks (M1), and proven in vivo by Tango's own
arrival on the showcase roster (§Relationships for that surface's
governance).

**Tango itself.** Tango is described on every tracked surface in its own
terms (P2): a contemporary, product-led identity whose character is set by
fidelity to its own reference (owner-private per P2) — under R15,
distinctness from Oak, PDS, and EMC² is a **consequence** of that
fidelity, never the objective; only EMC² maximises distance as its brief.
Light and dark are first-class; high-contrast and colour-safe are authored
by us (P5). Its design work follows the lane's standing disciplines: a
public genre survey of related design (light and dark exemplars) before
value authoring; narrow-first (R3/R4) — and per P6 (dated 2026-08-17)
Tango's character is clearly visible AT narrow (layout, order, spacing,
feel, distinct from Oak and PDS), a fidelity consequence the system must
not flatten; fluid values by construction (R14);
every expressive value deliberately distinct from any reference under
inspection (the DDR-007 deliberate-distinctness principle, generalised);
motion and tilt stances declared as per-identity data on the surfaces
named in the manifest facts. Derivation notes live in
`.agent/reference-local/` (untracked, machine-local — verified present at
the primary checkout 2026-08-17; the executing seat verifies access at T3
pickup and cards the owner if absent); the pack's tracked DECISIONS carry
Tango's design rationale in its own terms.

**Composition needs are measured here, built elsewhere (P4).** R15's
conditional directive stands on this page: where an identity's ratified
brief needs expression the system cannot give, the system changes. Ruling
5 is the owner's later, more specific word on *how* for composition:
capability + per-identity choice, **sequenced by Tango's measured needs**
— sequencing, not refusal. So this node measures: where Tango's design
wants composition divergence the kit cannot express (the seven
inexpressibles recorded in the critical-analysis report), T4 records each
concrete need. The record's reader is the author of the
composition-capability delivery node at ITS pickup, and T4 closes the
record with a disposition — either that node is proposed to the owner
with the measured needs as its warrant, or "no unexpressible needs found"
is recorded and nothing is proposed. Dated sharpening (P6, 2026-08-17,
incl. the same-sitting ontology correction): every identity's narrow
composition follows from its own character — there is no shared narrow
base and no default; Oak and PDS composing similarly at narrow is a
coincidence of their shared GDS-rooted public-service heritage, never a
base-sharing arrangement. Tango and EMC² are owner-directed toward
clear narrow distinctness (layout, order, spacing, feel), so the
measurement serves a declared outcome bar and Tango's design (T3/T4)
expresses its own narrow composition rather than inheriting anything.
The eventual-state requirement that the system FULLY support narrow
distinctness makes the seven inexpressibles' narrow-side items
needed-by-declared-outcome at the eventual state; sequencing is
unchanged (by Tango's measured needs).

**The flash-risk boundary, both ways.** Tango's per-theme polarity is
declared manifest data here (fact 2); the W3 switching-control work owns
the re-skin coalescing mechanism and its SC 2.3.1 falsifier cell — and
that cell must derive its worst-case polarity sequence from the installed
roster, not a pinned three-identity assumption, because this node makes
roster composition data rather than source.

**Rendered checkpoints bind early.** The first Tango pixels render at T2
from the real pack path — structure first, then design to wow — so every
subsequent checkpoint exercises the shipping mechanism, and the owner sees
pixels before value authoring begins.
