# Effectiveness and Impact

**Last Updated**: 9 July 2026

Planning hub for how Oak **knows its agent-facing surfaces work and deliver value**: evaluation
and assessment methodology, review and eval protocols for the MCP agent-facing content corpus,
and — downstream — the evidence chain from internal assurance to real-world impact.

Owner-named collection (2026-07-09). It sits between two existing bodies of doctrine and owns the
methodology layer connecting them:

- **Upstream doctrine**: [`validation-strategy.md`](../../directives/validation-strategy.md)
  (test/evaluate/assure spine; assurance tiers) and `principles.md` §Agentic Quality — this
  collection's research fills that strategy's deliberately deferred operational method.
- **Downstream evidence bar**: the
  [launch-readiness framework](../curriculum-mcp-path-to-ga/launch-readiness-framework.md)
  (K1: "live" = evidence state, not deploy state) and the tier-3 real-world value loop
  (implementation gated on the `mcp-product-analytics` lane promotion).

Scope boundary with neighbours: `agentic-engineering-enhancements` owns the Practice-layer evals
regime (skills/prompts/sub-agents, e.g. the skill-evals pilot); `sdk-and-mcp-enhancements` owns
what the MCP servers *do*. This collection owns **how the agent-facing content and its delivered
behaviour are assessed** — methodology, protocols, coverage, and the impact evidence chain.
