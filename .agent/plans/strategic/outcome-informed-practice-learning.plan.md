# Outcome-informed Practice learning

## Outcome

Experience from human-agent work becomes scoped, reversible Practice learning
that demonstrably improves later human-led work. People remain able to
understand, challenge, redirect, or retire what the Practice has learned. A
local incident cannot silently become a universal rule, and maintaining the
learning substrate does not become the dominant work.

Evidence enters the learning loop with its provenance, limits, custody,
decision rights, and privacy or safeguarding obligations intact. Missing,
stale, weak, or conflicting evidence remains visible. A later outcome—not the
mere production of a rule, document, or metric—judges whether the learning paid
off.

## The bet

The Practice already captures, refines, graduates, and enforces experience. The
FRAME-1 bet is that this meta-learning loop becomes more valuable and less
self-sealing when a bounded learning decision carries three separate contracts:
the capability and operational consequence; the intended effect and its
assumptions; and the feedback that can confirm, challenge, narrow, or retire the
learning.

The contracts keep the portable lesson distinct from the local incident and
host mechanism, expose who may collect and interpret evidence, and bind the
strength and reach of a Practice change to the evidence that warrants it.
Truthfulness, rigour, and accountability remain invariant qualities; each
present rule, gate, ceremony, and implementation remains corrigible. Human
leadership means legible judgement, challenge, correction, and appeal rather
than an automatic increase in approval gates.

This node deliberately does not bet on a broad agent-tools package split, a
single universal registry, a KPI for every capability, or one more layer of
coordination ceremony. Those choices require bounded evidence that they improve
the end-to-end effect. The companion purpose and negative-space exploration
records the current warrants, counter-evidence, and falsifiers.

## Success looks like

- Contrasting work journeys are selected from a declared population by a
  predeclared rule that includes smooth work, correction, failure, silent
  abandonment, and quiet success; results are compared with the simpler current
  decision process.
- Each learning decision exposed by those journeys has proportionate
  capability, effect-hypothesis, and feedback contracts. Evidence records
  provenance and coverage, freshness and expiry, missing-data meaning,
  confidence and known confounders, prohibited uses, and the interim rule while
  evidence is unavailable.
- Consequential decisions distinguish who may authorise collection, interpret
  evidence, interrupt or veto, accept risk, challenge or appeal, and resolve
  conflict. Custody alone grants none of those rights.
- People-derived evidence has a separately approved purpose and lawful-basis
  assessment, minimisation, access, retention and erasure, permitted-use,
  supplier-transfer, and safeguarding boundary before collection or secondary
  processing. Workplace evidence is transparent, impact-screened, and never
  used for covert monitoring, performance or disciplinary action, or individual
  profiling.
- Content-free aggregates are preferred by construction but are not assumed
  anonymous: sparse cohorts and rare combinations are suppressed, individual
  drill-down is unavailable, and re-identifiable aggregates remain personal
  data. This FRAME-1 node does not use or transfer pupil-level data anywhere.
- A local lesson reaches broader Practice only through proportionate
  corroboration, a scoped trial, preserved dissent, a bounded blast radius, and
  an explicit rollback or retirement path.
- Conserved learning demonstrably reduces recurrence, improves a later
  decision or outcome, or reduces material uncertainty without increasing
  human attention or total resource burden beyond the gain.

## Boundaries

This sketch does not claim that the learning loop is already observable or
select any package, build, publication, schema, or process change. It does not
assume that every worthwhile effect is quantifiable, and it governs no work
before owner ratification.

This FRAME-1 node does not govern operational continuity, FRAME-2 openness and
external adoption, FRAME-3 internal tool reuse, or product and mission outcomes.
Those can supply or receive evidence at the system boundary, but each needs its
own strategic warrant and failure model. No people-derived data collection,
linking, or secondary processing is authorised here; privacy, security, and
safeguarding review precedes any such proposal. Any future pupil-data case is
outside this node and requires separate safeguarding authority.

The bet is disconfirmed if a bounded set of contrasting work journeys shows
that, compared with the simpler current process, the three-contract model does
not improve a later outcome or decision, reduce recurrence or material
uncertainty, or if maintaining it costs more attention than the gain. In that
case this node should be narrowed or retired.

## Delivery

Delivery plans serving this node declare `serves: outcome-informed-practice-learning`
and are enumerated by search, never by a hand-kept list. Any such plan must name
the live decision it enables, the evidence that warrants intervention, its
falsifier or stop condition, and the beneficiary-level effect it expects to
change. It must isolate a narrower hypothesis or one feedback loop rather than
claim this strategic frame as sufficient warrant, and it must state any
information-governance and decision-rights obligations before evidence is
collected. Milestones live in the repository's designated execution system as
observable states; this strategic node does not mirror implementation tasks.
