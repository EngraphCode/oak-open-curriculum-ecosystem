# Outcome-informed Practice learning

## Kernel

> "The system's missing centre is outcome-informed human learning, not
> tooling neatness."
>
> — Possum turns Nocturne's closeout insight, 2026-08-01, from the
> agent-tools purpose-first architecture review

FRAME-1 (the purpose-first strategic sketch, explicitly unratified,
merged as evidence in PR #688) frames the Practice's work as
outcome-and-feedback contracts across four timescales. FRAME-1 itself is
a sketch and cannot be served by delivery plans; this node is its
ratified in-graph representative, so that delivery- and inquiry-tier work
steering by outcomes has a strategic parent. Ratifying this node does
NOT ratify FRAME-1: the sketch keeps its own status and its own
no-collection / no-implementation bounds.

## Outcome

Experience from human-agent work becomes scoped, reversible Practice
learning that demonstrably improves later human-led work. People remain
able to understand, challenge, redirect, or retire what the Practice has
learned. A local incident cannot silently become a universal rule, and
maintaining the learning substrate does not become the dominant work.

Evidence enters the learning loop with its provenance, limits, custody,
decision rights, and privacy or safeguarding obligations intact.
Missing, stale, weak, or conflicting evidence remains visible. A later
outcome—not the mere production of a rule, document, or metric—judges
whether the learning paid off.

## The bet

The Practice already captures, refines, graduates, and enforces
experience. The FRAME-1 bet is that this meta-learning loop becomes more
valuable and less self-sealing when a bounded learning decision carries
three separate contracts: the capability and operational consequence;
the intended effect and its assumptions; and the feedback that can
confirm, challenge, narrow, or retire the learning.

The contracts keep the portable lesson distinct from the local incident
and host mechanism, expose who may collect and interpret evidence, and
bind the strength and reach of a Practice change to the evidence that
warrants it. Truthfulness, rigour, and accountability remain invariant
qualities; each present rule, gate, ceremony, and implementation remains
corrigible. Human leadership means legible judgement, challenge,
correction, and appeal rather than an automatic increase in approval
gates.

This node deliberately does not bet on a broad agent-tools package
split, a single universal registry, a KPI for every capability, or one
more layer of coordination ceremony. Those choices require bounded
evidence that they improve the end-to-end effect. The companion purpose
and negative-space exploration records the current warrants,
counter-evidence, and falsifiers.

## What serves this node

Work whose deliverable is the estate learning whether its investments
produce their intended effects:

- The architecture-review inquiry strand (Possum's reports, Caracal's
  bounded inquiries) — each inquiry names an evidence ceiling, an impact
  bridge, and falsifiers, and tests whether a description changes a live
  decision.
- Effect-hypothesis and feedback-contract instruments attached to
  capability work — for example, the Subagent Invocation Framework's
  trial-window telemetry (close events, pre-registered priors, the
  diversity null hypothesis) REPORTS INTO this strand while Sif itself
  serves `agent-platform-citizenship`: capability frameworks live under
  the capability strand; whether they work is answered here.
- Doctrine disposition reviews that classify accepted obligations as
  enacted, deliberately deferred with a trigger, or retired — closing
  the loop Caracal's inquiry C named as the Practice's weak link.

## Success looks like

- Contrasting work journeys are selected from a declared population by a
  predeclared rule that includes smooth work, correction, failure,
  silent abandonment, and quiet success; results are compared with the
  simpler current decision process.
- Each learning decision exposed by those journeys has proportionate
  capability, effect-hypothesis, and feedback contracts. Evidence
  records provenance and coverage, freshness and expiry, missing-data
  meaning, confidence and known confounders, prohibited uses, and the
  interim rule while evidence is unavailable.
- Consequential decisions distinguish who may authorise collection,
  interpret evidence, interrupt or veto, accept risk, challenge or
  appeal, and resolve conflict. Custody alone grants none of those
  rights.
- People-derived evidence has a separately approved purpose and
  lawful-basis assessment, minimisation, access, retention and erasure,
  permitted-use, supplier-transfer, and safeguarding boundary before
  collection or secondary processing. Workplace evidence is transparent,
  impact-screened, and never used for covert monitoring, performance or
  disciplinary action, or individual profiling.
- Content-free aggregates are preferred by construction but are not
  assumed anonymous: sparse cohorts and rare combinations are
  suppressed, individual drill-down is unavailable, and re-identifiable
  aggregates remain personal data. This FRAME-1 node does not use or
  transfer pupil-level data anywhere.
- A local lesson reaches broader Practice only through proportionate
  corroboration, a scoped trial, preserved dissent, a bounded blast
  radius, and an explicit rollback or retirement path.
- Conserved learning demonstrably reduces recurrence, improves a later
  decision or outcome, or reduces material uncertainty without
  increasing human attention or total resource burden beyond the gain.

## Boundaries

This node does not claim that the learning loop is already observable,
and it selects no package, build, publication, schema, or process
change. It does not assume that every worthwhile effect is
quantifiable.

This FRAME-1 node does not govern operational continuity, FRAME-2
openness and external adoption, FRAME-3 internal tool reuse, or product
and mission outcomes. Those can supply or receive evidence at the
system boundary, but each needs its own strategic warrant and failure
model. No people-derived data collection, linking, or secondary
processing is authorised here; privacy, security, and safeguarding
review precedes any such proposal. Any future pupil-data case is
outside this node and requires separate safeguarding authority.

The bet is disconfirmed if a bounded set of contrasting work journeys
shows that, compared with the simpler current process, the
three-contract model does not improve a later outcome or decision,
reduce recurrence or material uncertainty, or if maintaining it costs
more attention than the gain. In that case this node should be narrowed
or retired.

## Delivery

Delivery plans serving this node declare
`serves: outcome-informed-practice-learning` and are enumerated by
search, never by a hand-kept list. Any such plan must name the live
decision it enables, the evidence that warrants intervention, its
falsifier or stop condition, and the beneficiary-level effect it expects
to change. It must isolate a narrower hypothesis or one feedback loop
rather than claim this strategic frame as sufficient warrant, and it
must state any information-governance and decision-rights obligations
before evidence is collected. Milestones live in the repository's
designated execution system as observable states; this strategic node
does not mirror implementation tasks.
