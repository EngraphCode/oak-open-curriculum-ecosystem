## 1. What the re-founding was

The resonance planning estate had grown **historically** — plans organised
by where work came from (transplant tranches, dated commissions, per-lane
workstreams), not by where it was going. Two measured harms: every reader
paid a translation tax, and plan prose drifted from repo truth unnoticed.
The re-founding (steps r1–r7, one owner commission, ~17 hours wall-clock
across two days, complete and owner-ratified) rebuilt the estate around
**destination**, with traceability running both ways:

- **Downward (intent → action)**: one owner-ratified verbatim north-star
  sentence at the head of the roadmap → four destination pillars (the
  strategy layer, boundaries owner-ratified) → one executable plan per
  pillar → typed todos, each carrying a machine-recomputable proof.
- **Upward (action → intent)**: any todo names its pillar; the pillar plan
  carries a provenance block naming exactly what it supersedes, the frozen
  originals, and its roadmap boundary; the roadmap names the ratification
  record. One hop per level, no dead ends.
- **Backward (new estate → old estate)**: a conservation ledger maps every
  inventory line of the retired estate into the new one (or to an explicit
  disposition), against byte-frozen originals.

The companion doctrine (already in your Box from the 2026-07-05 teaching
bundle): recorded plan state is a cache, never the truth — every todo
carries one of six proof kinds (artifact / gate / probe / git-fact /
ratified / attested), so "done" is a recomputation, not a claim.

## 2. The method, step by step, with each step's proof obligation

- **r1 — Freeze.** A MECHANICAL source rule (every plan-lane file, the
  roadmap, every next-session brief — no subjective "which files matter"
  filter; that filter was named the single biggest conservation risk at
  review). Verbatim copies into a dated archive directory; byte-identity
  proven per file (`diff` transcript recorded); every live original
  bannered "under re-founding" so no mid-transformation reader mistakes it
  for an execution queue. The freeze creates the **stable denominator**
  every later proof divides by (15 sources, 4,452 lines there).
- **r2 — Two-verdict audit.** The frozen corpus becomes a line-level
  inventory (three overlapping mechanical extraction nets: headings/
  frontmatter, list/table rows, a fixed keyword pattern), then every
  work-bearing item gets a state verdict probed in BOTH divergence
  directions: claimed-done must produce an artefact/gate proof;
  claimed-pending gets a did-it-already-land probe. Resonance's result: 7
  divergences, all "done but recorded pending", 0 the other way — plans
  under-report completion, so the repo, not the plans, is the source of
  truth. A **residue audit** proved the nets' blind spot empty: every
  un-captured prose line clusters into blocks anchored to a captured line,
  and the detector was proven to fire on a planted synthetic orphan before
  its zero was accepted.
- **r3 — Destination layer, owner-ratified live.** The north-star wording
  fixed VERBATIM at an interactive owner walk; pillar boundaries ratified
  as **audit-adjusted** (one pillar shrank by the proven-done set). The
  intent layer is the owner's words, not an agent's summary of them.
- **r4 — Author the pillar plans.** Every todo proof-typed; two-way
  banners from the moment each plan lands (new plan names what it
  supersedes; each superseded original points at its successor; first-read
  surfaces get interim pointers) so the coexistence window never routes a
  fresh reader to the old estate unsigned.
- **r5 — Conservation ledger + adversarial challenge.** Every inventory
  line mapped into the new estate with one of five dispositions
  (named-home / already-complete / merged-into / superseded-because /
  owner-rejected). The ledger is an **exact tiling**: 223 block rows tile
  all 1,443 inventory lines, zero gaps, zero overlaps, script-recomputed
  and independently recomputed by a critic. Then fresh-context challengers
  attacked ALL rows — the clean ones, not just the flagged ones. Result:
  zero overturns, but 33 real "weakens" (right disposition, dropped spec
  detail), cured structurally by a **frozen-spec binding clause**:
  conserved todos execute against their frozen spec sections, which remain
  the authoritative detail contracts.
- **r6 — Repoint before retire, one commit.** Every consumer of the old
  paths re-derived at execution (the authored consumer table treated as an
  accelerant, not an input), repointed by a small closed Decision Table
  (work-reference → pillar todo; record-reference → frozen copy; name-only
  historical prose → leave), durable provenance pointers landed on the
  three first-read surfaces, and only then the originals retired — all as
  ONE commit so the link gates only ever evaluate a finished state.
- **r7 — Owner walk + closure.** The owner walked the new estate against
  live-derived facts (todo counts recomputed at walk time, not read from
  records); six rulings, ratified; the governing plan, runbook, and thread
  brief self-archived.

## 3. Why "nothing lost" is provable there — the chain

Each link is independently auditable; together they close:

mechanical freeze rule → byte-identity proof → line-level denominator →
two-verdict state per line → tiling ledger over every line (zero gaps or
overlaps, recomputed twice) → fresh-context adversarial challenge of every
mapping → repoint-before-retire → gate-enforced one-commit landing →
post-commit re-proof.

Three design properties do the real work:

1. **Mechanical rules, not judgement, define the input set** (freeze rule,
   nets, denominator) — so completeness is checkable arithmetic.
2. **Every checker proves it can fail before its green is accepted** —
   the planted synthetic orphan for the residue detector, mutation probes
   for state gates, deliberate-break transcripts for quality gates. A zero
   from a detector that was never shown to fire is not a finding.
3. **Verification is layered by what each layer can uniquely catch** —
   mechanical recomputation catches count/byte drift; fresh-context
   adversarial challenge catches semantic loss (concept kept, spec detail
   dropped; a repointed file that now lies to its reader). Resonance
   measured this: the adversarial passes produced essentially all the new
   truth; the mechanical layers were cheap insurance that twice caught
   worker fabrication.

## 4. What went well (worth copying as-is)

- **Freeze-first with a stable denominator.** Every later claim divides by
  the same number; disputes resolve by recomputation.
- **The two-verdict audit before any authoring.** Authoring on unverified
  state was structurally impossible; the audit also shrank the work (a
  whole proven-done set was NOT re-planned, recorded in "Already Done —
  named with proof" tables instead).
- **Owner gates as the spine, everything else delegated.** Owner moments:
  commission, the r3 wording/boundary walk, a handful of mid-flight
  rulings, the r7 walk. All agent work in between, all verifiable.
- **Challenging the CLEAN mappings.** Zero overturns + 33 weakens is the
  signature result: the survey layer gets concepts right and drops detail;
  only fresh-context attack finds the drops.
- **Repoint-before-retire as one commit.** The gates never see a broken
  intermediate state; a missed consumer red-gates the landing by design.
- **Additive supersession notes** for history-vs-usefulness tension: a
  dated blockquote routing readers to the new home while historical text
  stays verbatim. Rewriting history and leaving it misleading were both
  wrong; annotation was the third way.
- **Coordination as a delivery multiplier**: three seats, one checkout, one
  day, zero collisions — claims registry + all-channels watcher + declared
  commit windows.

## 5. What to improve — the hyper-efficiency kit

Everything below is grounded in the participants' own recorded friction and
candidate cures (verified first-hand against the artefacts before this file
was authored). Ranked by expected saving for a second run:

1. **Script the mechanical layers; spend no LLM tokens on them.** The
   extraction nets are regex classes; the tiling proof is arithmetic; the
   byte-identity proof is `diff`. Resonance ran extraction through
   worker subagents under a rejection-heavy verification protocol: 74
   dispatches for 45 tasks, ~26 first-round rejections, 10 tasks pulled
   in-session anyway — and the measured conclusion was that worker
   extraction fidelity is length-correlated, workers occasionally
   fabricate, and the dispatcher's own recomputation was always the
   binding proof. A deterministic script produces the same inventory
   exactly, with the residue audit + planted-orphan discrimination kept
   as-is. (Their context: an owner ruling and a live-session token-headroom
   argument justified the fan-out THERE; a fresh run has no such
   constraint.) Reserve agents for judgement (dispositions) and attack
   (challenges); reserve the owner for ratification.
2. **Bind the frozen spec at authoring, not after the challenge pass.**
   The 33-weakens cure (frozen-spec binding clause) is now a known shape:
   author pillar todos as concept + typed proof + a binding clause citing
   the frozen spec section as the detail contract, from the first draft.
   Expect the challenge pass to then find genuinely little — and keep it
   anyway; it is the only layer that finds semantic loss.
3. **Make freeze and denominator one atomic commit, and re-derive the
   denominator at every merge into the working branch.** Resonance's one
   post-freeze arrival (a brief merged in from the main branch mid-arc)
   was caught by an adversarial critic — recomputation would have caught
   it for free.
4. **Author every probe as a closed sanctioned-survivor set, never as
   "returns nothing".** Three r6 probe frictions were one class: the
   runbook's own quoted paths self-matched whole-tree greps; files created
   mid-execution surprised the probes; legitimate archive references made
   the zero unprovable. Template-level cures: instruction artefacts exclude
   themselves from their own probes by construction; a step that creates a
   file amends downstream probe expectations in the same delta; probes
   assert "all survivors are in the named exception set".
5. **Verify tool/gate contracts at author time.** Two r6 mid-execution
   surprises were false beliefs about the estate's own tooling: the secret
   scanner scans HISTORY (so a "dead" allowlist line was a permanent
   history contract; retiring files never retires history), and the link
   validator excluded the archive from its inventory (so live→archive
   markdown links were structurally forbidden). One probe of each gate's
   actual behaviour at plan-author time converts a mid-flight owner
   escalation into a design fact. Generalise: before deleting any
   suppression, probe what the scanner actually scans; dry-run every
   literal replacement text through the estate's validators.
6. **Cite by stable ids from the first draft, never by line numbers.**
   The ledger's line-number citations into the live pillar plans went
   stale within hours (the cure inserts moved the lines). Stable todo ids
   cost nothing and never rot.
7. **Give critics the unit definition and untruncated inputs.** Two of
   nine completeness-critic findings were artefacts of missing context
   (it lacked the anchor-unit definition; it saw a truncated slice).
   A critic's brief is decision-complete or its findings are noise.
8. **Pre-declare the accretion policy for the coexistence window.** The
   new estate legitimately grew between authoring and ratification
   (sanctioned owner additions); naming upfront what may accrete into
   not-yet-ratified plans, and under whose ruling, makes the ratification
   walk's deltas expected rather than explained.
9. **Build the plan-state recomputation tool FIRST if you can.** Resonance
   specified proof-typed recomputable state (their PDR-129/130) during the
   arc but built the tool after; with the tool first, the state audit
   becomes a recomputation, the ratification walk consumes tool output,
   and the estate is born gated instead of born audited. This is the
   single largest structural saving available to a second runner.
10. **Keep a metaloss discipline at handoffs.** When declaring anything
    "not conserved", cite the sub-reason — (a) re-derivable from
    structure, (b) forbidden to conserve, (c) conjecture (cure: harden the
    artefact, not conserve the prediction). A declaration citing its
    sub-reason is checkable; a bare "not conserved" is not.

## 6. A candidate shape for this estate (exploration seed, not a plan)

The portable skeleton, with the estate-specific seams named:

- **Seam 1 — the source set.** Your planning surfaces differ (this estate
  carries `plans/`, `plans-old-archive/`, `milestones/`, `proposals/`, and
  report/analysis surfaces). The mechanical freeze rule must be derived
  from YOUR lanes; the pre-existing old-archive is a Wave-0 sweep input
  (non-terminal concepts hiding in archived files), not a freeze source.
- **Seam 2 — the intent layer.** Resonance's north star was a single
  owner sentence; this estate has VISION.md and a milestones surface. The
  r3-equivalent is whatever owner-ratified wording your estate treats as
  canonical intent — the method only requires that it be verbatim,
  owner-ratified, and homed at the head of the destination carrier.
- **Seam 3 — the proof estate.** The two-verdict audit probes against
  YOUR gates/CI/git facts; the proof-kind taxonomy is already portable
  (your Box carries it).
- **Seam 4 — live-lane coordination.** Freeze copies nothing out from
  under an active seat; repoints on surfaces a live lane owns are
  coordinated on your stream (resonance ran three concurrent seats through
  this without collisions).
- **Sizing prior**: resonance's estate was 15 sources / 4,452 lines /
  1,443 inventory lines / 223 ledger rows, ~17h wall-clock including owner
  gates, with the agent spend concentrated in the adversarial passes. A
  scripted-mechanical-layer run should spend materially less than their
  ~1.3M+ subagent tokens on verification waves while keeping the
  adversarial budget intact.
