# Napkin

## Session: 2026-07-19 to 2026-07-20 - Oak App Foundations

### What Was Done

- Mapped OWA and Oak Components recursively and through 53 orthogonal concept
  lenses, then reduced the portfolio to a mechanism-neutral conceptual basis.
- Mapped OCE as the target substrate without granting its present boundaries
  architectural authority.
- Mapped Database-Tools through oak-openapi into OCE using 59 further lenses,
  executable inventories, a contract comparison and adversarial source review.
- Converted owner corrections into governing research policy and falsifiable
  hypotheses rather than treating them as local wording changes.

### Surprise

- **Expected:** the materialized view would be the principal upstream boundary
  relevant to OCE.
- **Actual:** the public product composes multiple versioned projections,
  ordinary tables/views, handler joins, Redis policy state, CMS material, bulk
  infrastructure and manual/generated contract representations.
- **Why expectation failed:** consumption topology was mistaken for semantic
  authority, and one named dependency hid several independent clocks and owners.
- **Behaviour change:** trace every consequential claim through authority,
  release, projection, contract and capability; never promote a convenient read
  model to domain authority without evidence.

### Surprise

- **Expected:** exact path/schema equality between the pinned provider document
  and OCE cache would settle the generation boundary.
- **Actual:** all 32 paths, GET operations and component schemas corresponded
  structurally, yet prose overlays, lossy capability compilation, binary/HTTP
  semantics and future acquisition remained separate claims.
- **Why expectation failed:** snapshot equality answers correspondence at one
  representation and time, not semantic completeness or process reliability.
- **Behaviour change:** state the protocol level and time horizon of every
  equality claim; test transformations for totality and keep authored capability
  semantics independently reviewable.

### Surprise

- **Expected:** pinned-link, structure and numerical inventory checks would make
  the large lens portfolio semantically reliable.
- **Actual:** independent review still found a reversed `52% missing` claim and
  over-broad rights/transcript conclusions even though source anchors resolved.
- **Why expectation failed:** reachability and structural validity do not test a
  claim's polarity, denominator, scope or evidence label.
- **Behaviour change:** pair mechanical anchor validation with an independent
  semantic claim audit that checks quantity, direction, qualification and source
  entailment before synthesis is accepted.

### Owner And Platform Corrections

- The user is Oak and is the decision authority for genuine scope, identity and
  value forks. Surface those questions immediately as explicit action cards;
  continue making routine reversible execution choices autonomously.
- OWA and Components reveal what must be understood, not what must be copied or
  fixed. OCE architecture starts from outcomes, obligations, premise challenge
  and excellence.
- Excellence is the only governing priority. Lower maintenance and innovation
  effort are consequences of coherent excellent design, never prior constraints.
- The kit is deliberate infrastructure for additional consumers. Existing
  consumer count is not a rule for whether a framework boundary may exist.

### Disposition

The settled substance above lives in the research charter, research practice,
