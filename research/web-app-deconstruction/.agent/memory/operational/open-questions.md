# Open Questions

This register is for strategic questions that shape future work, are not cheaply
answerable from current evidence and have no current decisive answer.

The register is empty. Research unknowns and discriminating questions are owned
by the [investigation backlog](../../../docs/investigations/backlog.md),
[validation register](../../../docs/investigations/validation-register.md) and
the relevant current-state or premise record; they are not duplicated here.
