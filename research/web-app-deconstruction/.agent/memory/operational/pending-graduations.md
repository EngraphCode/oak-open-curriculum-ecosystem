# Pending Graduations

This register is for settled architectural or research-practice doctrine whose
permanent destination is known but not yet written.

The register is empty. Current architecture options and hypotheses are not
settled doctrine; they remain in the
[option register](../../../docs/investigations/architecture-option-register.md)
and [hypothesis register](../../../docs/hypotheses/README.md) until their
invalidators and evidence justify a decision.
