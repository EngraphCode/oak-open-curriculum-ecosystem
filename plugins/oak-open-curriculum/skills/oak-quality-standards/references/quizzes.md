# Oak quality standards — Quizzes (starter and exit)

Standards for reviewing a **quiz** — the prior-knowledge starter and the exit quiz.

**114 standards** — 72 Required (must), 42 Model Practice (should). Judge each against its wording: Met / Partially met / Not met / Not applicable, with evidence and a fix. Required failures are blocking; Model Practice gaps are recommendations.
