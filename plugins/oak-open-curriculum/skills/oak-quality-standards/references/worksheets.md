# Oak quality standards — Worksheets and worksheet answers

Standards for reviewing a **worksheet** (and its answers document).

**116 standards** — 53 Required (must), 63 Model Practice (should). Judge each against its wording: Met / Partially met / Not met / Not applicable, with evidence and a fix. Required failures are blocking; Model Practice gaps are recommendations.
