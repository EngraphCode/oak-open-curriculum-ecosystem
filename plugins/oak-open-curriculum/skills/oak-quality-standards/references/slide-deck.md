# Oak quality standards — Slide deck

Standards for reviewing a lesson **slide deck**.

**281 standards** — 112 Required (must), 169 Model Practice (should). Judge each against its wording: Met / Partially met / Not met / Not applicable, with evidence and a fix. Required failures are blocking; Model Practice gaps are recommendations.
