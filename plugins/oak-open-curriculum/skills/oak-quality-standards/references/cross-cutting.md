# Oak quality standards — Cross-cutting standards (apply across components)

**Cross-cutting** standards with no single component — accessibility, safeguarding, spelling/grammar, and whole-lesson expectations. Check these for every review.

**37 standards** — 11 Required (must), 26 Model Practice (should). Judge each against its wording: Met / Partially met / Not met / Not applicable, with evidence and a fix. Required failures are blocking; Model Practice gaps are recommendations.
