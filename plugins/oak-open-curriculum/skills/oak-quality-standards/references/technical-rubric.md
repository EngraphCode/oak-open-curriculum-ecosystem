# Oak Technical Rubric

The technical rubric is a framework for assessing whether a lesson is of suitably high quality. Its descriptors are **high-tariff, measurable** questions covering all aspects of a lesson (general) and specific components. Each question lists the measures used to judge it. It does not measure every aspect of technical quality, and is used alongside the **Pedagogical Rubric** (`pedagogical-rubric.md`).

Rubric codes here match the `Rubric code` shown against each standard in the component reference files.

## General

### TG1 — Does the lesson follow Oak's style and presentation guidelines?

- All resources align to Oak templates and branding.
- All content follows technical, and subject-specific, correct spelling, punctuation and grammar.
- All essential content sits inside the left, right and the lowest grid line.
- All resource titles, subheadings and tasks match throughout the lesson.
- Resource is titled `Lesson UID - Lesson Title - Resource Type`.
- All non-essential content is removed (copy-and-paste-ables, speaker notes, comments, PiP outline).

### TG2 — Is the lesson accessible?

- Font is Lexend, Kalam, or Abeezee (only for numbers or formula), at an appropriate size for the resource type: slide deck text min 18; slide deck table min 14; worksheet/answers primary min 14, secondary min 11; additional material min 12.
- Text is left aligned (exemption: array images in MFL).
- No use of the underline or italic function.
- All text must be editable.
- Images are high-resolution and non-pixelated.
- Media clips work as expected when clicked and carry the appropriate icon within each resource; the video icon / audio icon is included wherever a clip is used; clips are linked to the correct URL.
- If media clips are used, the yellow media clips teacher guidance slide is included directly after the universal teacher guidance slide.
- Lesson resources complement each other but can also be used independently.

### TG3 — Is the lesson format suitable?

- The format of lesson resources is not affected when downloaded: slide deck, worksheet, worksheet answers as PowerPoint; additional material as Word.

### TG4 — Has third-party content, including images, been used and attributed correctly?

- Where attribution is required it is displayed clearly within the resource (slide deck / worksheet / additional resources: below the bottom grid line, left aligned, size 6 dark grey, on the page the image appears; quizzes: alongside the image).
- Images requiring attribution are attributed throughout.
- Any supplementary media files are tagged to the lesson in the third-party content section of lesson view in the CAT.

### TG5 — Does the resource contain the Open Government Licence (OGL) statement?

- Lesson resources contain the OGL statement with partner name in line with the template.

## Lesson information

### TL1 — Does the lesson information accurately display the content of the lesson?

- The lesson information matches the content within the slides.
- Lesson outcome begins with "I can … " and ends with a full stop.

## Slides

### TS1 — Does the lesson content fit within the slide?

- All essential content sits inside the left, right or lowest grid lines.
- Animation appears in the correct order.
- Only "appear" and "disappear" animation is used.

### TS2 — Is the slide deck accessible?

- The use of bold is seen only for the keywords listed on the keywords slide (exemption: unbolded keywords where bolding would give away an answer).
- All links within the slide deck link out as anticipated (e.g. teacher guidance slide, video and/or audio).

### TS3 — Is the slide formatted correctly?

- Slide decks include a title slide at the start which displays the subject icon and subject area.
- The correct slide template is used for the content (e.g. CfU template for a CfU, practice template for a practice task).
- Practice tasks are demarcated A, B, C, etc. in the practice slide banner.
- Questions within practice tasks are numbered 1, 2, 3, etc.; sub-questions a, b, c; no further sectioning.
- Every lesson includes one lesson summary slide at the end of the deck.

## Quizzes

### TQ1 — Are the quizzes accessible?

- Quizzes are displayed as expected using preview mode.

### TQ2 — Are the question stems formatted correctly?

- Question stems support system integration: short answer uses `{{}}` where a response is required mid-sentence; multiple choice uses ten underscores for a gap fill mid-sentence and "…" for a gap fill at the end.
- All quiz questions include one piece of relevant feedback and one relevant hint.
- Question stems do not state the number of responses required.

## Worksheets and worksheet answers

### TW1 — Is the worksheet formatted correctly?

- Worksheet text is black to reduce printing costs.
- Worksheets mirror the practice tasks within the slide deck; worksheet answers mirror the feedback slide within the slide deck.
- Practice tasks are demarcated A, B, C; questions numbered 1, 2, 3 with sub-questions a, b, c; no further sectioning.

## Video

### TV1 — Is the video well presented?

- Videos should ideally be 20–35 minutes long.
- The first five seconds is the title slide (full screen), no narration or PiP.
- No unwanted pop-ups, notifications or control bars.
- PiP appears in at least 50% of the video.
- The OGL slide appears at the end and is held for five seconds.

### TV2 — Does the teacher present themselves well?

- Plain, neutral, non-blurred background where nothing personal or inappropriate can be seen or heard.
- The teacher is professionally and appropriately dressed for the subject in simple, plain clothes (no branding or slogans).

### TV3 — Is the video accessible?

- No age group / time / key stage / reference to other lessons' titles is made in the video.
- Clear, synchronised, unpixelated visuals; smooth transitions; no long sections of dead air.
- Synchronisation and recording quality is clear, with little to no background noise.
- Media clips are played within the video.

## Additional material

### TAM1 — Is the additional material formatted correctly?

- Contains an introductory paragraph explaining its function.
- Formatted in line with subject-specific formats.
- When there is an additional material this is reflected in the adapted hidden teacher slide.
- The use of bold is seen only for the keywords listed within the lesson.

## Computing

### TC1 — Are additional files accessible?

- Where an additional downloadable file is required, it is added in the "downloadable file" section of lesson view in the CAT.
- Additional downloadable files are provided in a universally accessible format: txt, csv, py and/or mp4.

> The PE parallel rubric uses `PE-`-prefixed codes (`PE-TG2`, `PE-TV1`, …) for the same questions adapted to practical PE; standards tagged ⚑PE carry those codes.
