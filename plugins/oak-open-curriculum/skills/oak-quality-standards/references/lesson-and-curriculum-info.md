# Oak quality standards — Lesson and curriculum information

Standards for the **lesson and curriculum information** (the structured data behind a lesson or unit).

**60 standards** — 60 Required (must), 0 Model Practice (should). Judge each against its wording: Met / Partially met / Not met / Not applicable, with evidence and a fix. Required failures are blocking; Model Practice gaps are recommendations.
