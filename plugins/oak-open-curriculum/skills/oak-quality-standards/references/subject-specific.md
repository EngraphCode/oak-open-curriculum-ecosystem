# Oak quality standards — Subject-specific standards

**Subject-specific** standards (MFL/Languages, Computing, RE, Music, Art and Design). Apply on top of the general standards for that subject.

**60 standards** — 28 Required (must), 32 Model Practice (should). Judge each against its wording: Met / Partially met / Not met / Not applicable, with evidence and a fix. Required failures are blocking; Model Practice gaps are recommendations.
